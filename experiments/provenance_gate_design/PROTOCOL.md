## Objective and frozen success criterion

Determine whether a representation-neutral provenance rule can accept the retained live 168-byte exception while continuing to reject the historical false links that the current namespace gate eliminated.

The rule may be considered supported only if it restores the retained live exception without restoring any previously eliminated false substantive exception.

A pass is bounded to this corpus and stated trust assumptions, not enterprise performance. A missing essential premise is NOT_DEMONSTRATED, not a pass or proof of impossibility. Restoring any eliminated false exception fails the safety condition. Zero accepted true and false exceptions demonstrates abstention, not improved discrimination. No in-corpus success establishes generalization to authentic misleading native evidence.

## Exact proposed rule P1

Evaluate each existing cited linkage edge independently, without creating links or modifying M6. Require all of:

1. **Producer identity:** evidence identifies the producer for each occurrence in this episode, distinguishing generator from observer. A brand or arbitrary issuer label is insufficient; trusted operator custody may identify a bounded local producer without cryptographic attestation.
2. **Observation role:** evidence binds the occurrence to a specific source field and acquisition/observation path, not just normalized text.
3. **Origin:** establish whether each occurrence was generated, received or forwarded, including upstream origin for received/forwarded values.
4. **Relationship semantics:** an applicable producer contract supports the exact relationship claimed (same runtime attempt, same server request/response). Identifier equality is at most a required consistency check after provenance is qualified; tuple similarity and product-name equality cannot establish the relationship. Received copies are not independent generators.
5. **Episode applicability:** retained operational evidence binds the relevant producer/configuration/code contract to the episode. No mandatory AGG schema or cryptographic mechanism: an existing execution/deployment/configuration record may suffice. Source co-location in a commit, compatible outputs and generic documentation alone do not establish which implementation/configuration executed. A newly assumed missing applicability edge cannot be counted as established.
6. **Custody/trust:** disclose source custodian, mutable/privileged paths and assumptions about faithful records and ordinary producer operation. These assumptions do not fill a missing observation or applicability binding. An AGG declaration may be recorded when it is the only evidence, but cannot authenticate itself or turn synthetic rows into native observations.
7. **Contradictions:** examine the retained native evidence relevant to origin, namespace and contract application. No contradictory evidence may remain unresolved; unexamined/inaccessible evidence is unknown, not absence. Absence of observed contradiction is bounded to the reviewed package, not global completeness.

Each requirement receives ESTABLISHED, CONTRACT_ONLY, ASSUMED, MISSING or CONTRADICTED with citations. Only ESTABLISHED is sufficient for requirements 1–5; requirement 6 additionally needs an explicit trust statement; requirement 7 needs completed bounded review with no unresolved contradiction. Synthetic producer-shaped records fail native producer qualification regardless of expected truth. Actual generator provenance may be established as the fixture generator, but that does not support a runtime/server relationship.

An exception is a design candidate only when all edges of at least one existing supporting path pass P1 and the existing scope, denied-effect, integrity and correlation prerequisites pass. This changes namespace evidence format only in the proposed design; it does not waive those prerequisites, relax frozen timing/coverage standards or execute a replacement M7 gate. The production default finding remains separately reported.
