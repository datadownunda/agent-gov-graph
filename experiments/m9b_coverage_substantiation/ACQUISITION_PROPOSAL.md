## Smallest useful test after source review

Preregister one candidate episode, its environment and exact operational sources. Preserve source-native bytes and their export context before deriving support statements. Use the unchanged control and public attestation entry point only after the source gate passes. A human-reviewed explanation may connect source observations to existing support fields; it must expose every inference and trust assumption rather than present authored booleans as additional evidence.

Test whether withholding a relevant rotation segment, truncating the export before the horizon, restricting export to one identity, or removing clock evidence causes the source review to reject adequacy. Make each challenge on a labelled scratch copy; preserve originals and avoid claiming that a constructed omission occurred in production. These challenges test review sensitivity. They do not prove authentic completeness against a privileged administrator who controls all supplied evidence.

Success would establish a defensible bounded finding for this single target episode and stated custody model. Failure to obtain scope, continuity, completion or clock evidence remains **NOT_DEMONSTRATED**. A discovered served representation within the prohibited horizon is evidence against effectiveness for that episode, subject to its correlation quality. Do not expand the boundary to client receipt, production enforcement generally, or all activity by the agent.
