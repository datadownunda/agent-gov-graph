## Verdicts and falsification

SUBSTANTIATED: stage 1 passes with ordinary native/source evidence and the replayed real episode supports adequate bounded coverage; all critical negative diagnostics abstain. NOT_DEMONSTRATED: stage 1 lacks required evidence or available evidence cannot justify adequacy, even if synthetic tests pass. FALSIFIED: a substantiated inadequacy in a required negative diagnostic yields CONTROL_EFFECTIVE, or an asserted live positive is contradicted by the native evidence; distinguish implementation defect from broader project thesis. NOT_EVALUABLE: operational/integrity failure prevents the specified assessment. Report stage-1 matrix, diagnostic outcomes and overall decision separately, not a pooled success rate.
